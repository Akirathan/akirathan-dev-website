Welcome to `akirathan.dev` — a website with:

- All my professional experience.
- My thoughts and opinions on various tech topics.

This whole website is not just my glorified CV.

## Why I built this website

- To help me sort out my thoughts about what I want to do next.
- Because I started to look for a new job.
- A [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pavel-marek-56a517140/) profile is not enough to show all my skills and experience.
- I wanted to fiddle with [WebGPU](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGPU_API), and there is no better place for experimentation than your own personal portfolio.
